# Zomato Restaurant Expansion Analysis | Excel

## Project Overview

This project explores restaurant and market data from Zomato to identify potential locations for business expansion. Using Excel, I analyzed 9,551 restaurant records across 15 countries to evaluate market opportunities based on restaurant density, customer ratings, and average cost.

The analysis applies a market scoring methodology to shortlist high-potential countries and cities, supporting data-driven decision-making for restaurant expansion.

## Business Objective

To identify markets that demonstrate potential for restaurant expansion by evaluating:

* Restaurant concentration and competition levels.
* Average customer ratings as an indicator of restaurant quality.
* Average cost as a measure of market pricing.
* Customer engagement and restaurant distribution across cities.

## Tools & Techniques

* **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, transformation, analysis, and visualization.
* **Pivot Tables:** Aggregation and comparison of restaurant metrics across countries and cities.
* **Excel formulas:** Calculations and market scoring.
* **Data visualization:** Charts and summaries to communicate findings.

## Methodology

1. **Data Preparation:** Reviewed and structured restaurant data to ensure consistency and usability.
2. **Exploratory Analysis:** Examined restaurant distribution, average ratings, and average cost across markets.
3. **Market Evaluation:** Developed a scoring methodology using restaurant count, average rating, and average cost.
4. **City-Level Analysis:** Identified cities with customer engagement indicators and relatively low restaurant density.
5. **Market Shortlisting:** Evaluated the results to shortlist five potential countries and five cities for further consideration.

## Key Findings

* Evaluated 9,551 restaurant records spanning 15 countries.
* Identified five high-potential countries and five cities through a structured market scoring approach.
* Highlighted markets with average ratings of 4.0 or above and relatively low competition.
* Identified cities demonstrating customer engagement alongside lower restaurant density.

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates how spreadsheet-based analysis can be used to evaluate market characteristics, identify potential expansion opportunities, and translate raw restaurant data into structured business insights.

**Note:** This is an academic analysis based on the available dataset. The shortlisted markets represent analytical opportunities and are not validated business expansion decisions.
